      "On" color
      The dot and fill on options that are switched on (captions On, effects picked, loop…).
      ExportSelected

      Click a shortcut, then press the key you want for it. Esc cancels, Backspace leaves an action without a key. A key can only do one thing: if the key you press already does something (another shortcut, or something Cutsona or Windows needs), Cutsona says what, and you can swap the keys, use it anyway or pick another. Keys 1 to 9 always pick a take.

      Cutsona is open source (MIT license) and built on free, open-source software. Thank you to everyone who makes and looks after these projects. Cutsona on GitHub

      Video and audio

      • FFmpegLGPL / GPL

        Reads, converts and saves every video and song: exports, previews, downloads and the waveform.

        GitHubffmpeg.orgWindows builds by gyan.dev
      • yt-dlpUnlicense

        Downloads clips and songs from YouTube, Twitch, Kick, TikTok and hundreds of other sites.

        GitHub
      • mp4-muxerMIT

        Packs the frames Cutsona renders into the MP4 files you export.

        GitHub

      Finding people and words

      • MediaPipeApache 2.0

        Finds faces and people in your clips so auto-framing can follow them (BlazeFace and EfficientDet-Lite models).

        GitHubWebsite
      • faster-whisperMIT

        Hears the words in your clips, for captions and for cutting between sentences.

        GitHub
      • WhisperMIT

        The speech recognition model faster-whisper runs, by OpenAI.

        GitHub
      • CTranslate2MIT

        Runs Whisper fast on your computer’s processor.

        GitHub

      What Cutsona runs on

      • ElectronMIT

        Makes Cutsona a desktop app: its window, its installer, and its updates (with electron-builder and electron-updater).

        GitHubWebsiteelectron-builder
      • Node.jsMIT

        Runs Cutsona’s engine: searching, downloading, and talking to FFmpeg and yt-dlp.

        GitHubnodejs.org
      • ChromiumBSD

        Draws the Cutsona window: video playback and WebGL effects.

        GitHubchromium.org
      • curlcurl license

        Fetches search results and files from the web.

        GitHubcurl.se
      • PythonPSF

        Runs faster-whisper for the word timing.

        GitHubpython.org
      • ArchivoSIL OFL 1.1

        The typeface of the Cutsona interface, by Omnibus-Type.

        GitHub

      Copyright check

        Cutsona judges each clip and song by where it came from and its license, and asks YouTube about its own videos. It can't hear or see the footage itself, so treat this as a guide: it can't promise a post won't be claimed.
